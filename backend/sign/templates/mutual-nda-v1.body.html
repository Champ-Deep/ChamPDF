RECITALS

A. The Parties wish to explore the following opportunity (the Purpose): {{purpose}}

B. In connection with the Purpose each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and each Party is willing to receive it on the terms of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Affiliate means, in relation to a Party, any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, and in relation to Champions includes Lake B2B, Champions Infometrics, SPAN Global Services, Ampliz, InfraTech, Aventura, Champions Ranch, Beach Cities, Champions Club, and any special purpose vehicle promoted or controlled by Champions.

1.2 Confidential Information means all information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the Disclosing Party) to the other Party (the Receiving Party) in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, pricing, client and prospect identities, methodologies, data samples, product roadmaps, financial information, and the existence and terms of the Parties' discussions.

1.3 Personal Data has the meaning given in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and includes any equivalent term under the data protection law applicable to a data subject.

1.4 Representatives means a Party's directors, officers, employees and professional advisers who are members of the deal team for the Purpose and who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

1.5 Trade Secrets means Confidential Information that derives independent economic value from not being generally known, including source data, scoring models, enrichment logic and client lists.

2. PURPOSE

2.1 The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose, and shall not use it to compete with, or to the detriment of, the Disclosing Party.

3. OBLIGATIONS OF CONFIDENCE

3.1 The Receiving Party shall keep Confidential Information strictly confidential and protect it with at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information, and in no case less than reasonable care.

3.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives, and only to the extent they need to know it for the Purpose. Internal circulation beyond the deal team is a disclosure requiring the Disclosing Party's prior written consent.

3.3 The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any agency, contractor, sub-processor or other third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent, and where consent is given shall procure that the third party is bound by written obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

3.4 The Receiving Party shall maintain a written log of every person and entity to whom Confidential Information has been disclosed and shall produce it to the Disclosing Party within five business days of a written request.

3.5 The Receiving Party is liable for any act or omission of its Representatives or permitted third parties that would breach this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

4. EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

(a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure, or later enters the public domain other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession before disclosure, free of any obligation of confidence;

(c) was lawfully obtained from a third party who was free to disclose it; or

(d) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information.

4.2 No residuals. Nothing in this Agreement permits the Receiving Party to use Confidential Information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel, and any purported "residuals" or "indistinguishable expertise" right is excluded.

4.3 A combination of features or items of information is not within an exclusion in Clause 4.1 merely because individual features or items are, unless the combination itself is.

5. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, where lawful, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, cooperate with any effort to obtain protective treatment, and disclose only the minimum required.

6. SAMPLES AND EVALUATION MATERIALS

6.1 Any sample data, sample files or evaluation materials provided in connection with the Purpose are Confidential Information, are provided for evaluation only, and shall not be loaded into any production system, marketed to, or contacted.

6.2 The Receiving Party shall securely delete all samples and evaluation materials, and certify deletion in writing, within {{schedule_sample_deletion}}.

6.3 Samples may be seeded with control records. Any contact with a control record by or on behalf of the Receiving Party is prima facie evidence of use in breach of this Agreement.

7. PERSONAL DATA

7.1 Confidential Information may include business contact data of individuals. Each Party shall process such Personal Data only for the Purpose, in compliance with applicable data protection law, and shall not transfer it outside the jurisdiction of receipt without the Disclosing Party's written consent.

7.2 Where any records or datasets are to move between the Parties beyond samples under Clause 6, the Parties shall first execute the Champions Database Non-Disclosure Agreement, and no such records shall move until it is signed.

8. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

8.1 The Receiving Party shall not input, upload or otherwise make Confidential Information available to any machine learning or artificial intelligence system for training, fine-tuning, embedding or retrieval, other than an enterprise system contractually bound not to train on inputs, and then only for the Purpose.

8.2 Any output, embedding or derivative generated from Confidential Information is itself Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party.

9. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

9.1 Within fifteen days of the Disclosing Party's written request or the end of the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information, including copies and derivatives, and certify this in writing, save that one archival copy may be retained by legal counsel solely to evidence compliance, subject to this Agreement.

10. NO LICENCE, NO WARRANTY

10.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

10.2 Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party makes any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither Party is obliged to enter into any further agreement.

11. INCIDENT NOTICE

11.1 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing within {{schedule_incident_notice}} of becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorised access to, use of, or disclosure of Confidential Information, and shall cooperate fully in investigating and remedying it.

12. TERM AND SURVIVAL

12.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for {{schedule_term}}, unless terminated earlier by either Party on thirty days' written notice.

12.2 The obligations in Clauses 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17 survive termination for three years from the date of termination. In respect of Trade Secrets and Personal Data the obligations survive for as long as the information remains a Trade Secret or Personal Data.

13. NON-SOLICITATION

13.1 For {{schedule_non_solicit}} from the Effective Date, neither Party shall directly or indirectly solicit for employment or engagement any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. General advertisements not targeted at such employees are not solicitation.

14. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

14.1 This Clause applies where Schedule 1 marks Non-circumvention as "yes" (currently: {{non_circumvention}}).

14.2 For twenty-four months from the Effective Date, the Receiving Party shall not, without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent, directly or indirectly approach, contract with or transact with any client, prospect, investor, landowner, broker, channel partner or other person introduced by the Disclosing Party in connection with the Purpose (an Introduced Party) so as to circumvent the Disclosing Party.

14.3 If the Receiving Party transacts with an Introduced Party in breach of Clause 14.2, it shall pay the Disclosing Party the fee or commission the Disclosing Party would have earned had the transaction been concluded through it, without prejudice to any other remedy.

14.4 A Party that had a documented prior relationship with a person before that person was introduced shall notify the other Party in writing within ten days of the introduction, failing which the person is deemed an Introduced Party.

15. PUBLICITY

15.1 Neither Party shall make any public announcement or use the other Party's name or marks in connection with the Purpose without the other Party's prior written consent.

16. REMEDIES

16.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which damages would be inadequate, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without proof of special damage, in addition to any other remedy.

16.2 Any limitation or exclusion of liability agreed between the Parties in any other document does not apply to a breach of this Agreement or to the unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information or Personal Data.
