Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube long-form videos and Shorts, plus Instagram posts and Reels. If your link works on those platforms and is publicly viewable, it should work here.

What's the MP3 quality?

Audio is encoded at 320 kbps CBR, stereo, 44.1 kHz. The actual quality depends on the source — if the original audio is lower bitrate, the MP3 won't gain detail it never had.

Are private or login-required videos supported?

No. Only publicly accessible content can be downloaded. Private posts, age-gated videos, and content that requires sign-in will return an error.

Are there length limits?

Videos up to 30 minutes are supported. Live streams and scheduled premieres are not.
